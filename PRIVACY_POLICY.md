# Privacy Policy for Tanpura Kings

**Last updated:** May 20, 2026

Kingsman Software Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") built the Tanpura Kings app (the "App") as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

### Microphone Access
The App includes a built-in chromatic tuner that uses your device's microphone to detect musical pitch in real time. **Audio is processed entirely on your device** — it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

### Advertising Data
The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, including:
- Device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA)
- IP address (for approximate location-based ad targeting)
- App usage and interaction data

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### App Preferences
The App stores your selected octave preference locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). This data never leaves your device.

## How We Use Information

- **Microphone data:** Solely for real-time pitch detection in the tuner. Never recorded or transmitted.
- **Advertising data:** To display relevant advertisements and support the free availability of the App.
- **Preferences:** To remember your settings between app sessions.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

- **Google AdMob** — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your Choices

- **Ad Tracking (iOS):** When you first open the App, you will be asked whether to allow tracking for personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **Ad Tracking (Android):** You can opt out of personalized ads in Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.
- **Microphone:** You can deny microphone access at any time in your device settings. The tuner feature will be unavailable, but the drone/tanpura functionality will work normally.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Retention

The App does not collect or store any personal data on our servers. All data remains on your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
